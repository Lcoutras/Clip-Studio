Clip Studio Privacy Policy

Clip Studio is a desktop app that runs on its user's own computer. It turns the user's own gameplay recordings into short clips and, when the user connects a TikTok account, uploads those clips to that account.

What it accesses

  • Your TikTok account's basic profile information and permission to upload videos. These are used only to upload the clips you export.
  • Your sign-in token. It is stored only on your own computer and sent only to TikTok.

What it doesn't do

  • It has no servers and collects no data. Nothing is sent to the developer or to third parties.
  • It doesn't sell, share or analyze your data.

Removing access

Delete the output\tiktok folder on your computer, and remove Clip Studio under TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
