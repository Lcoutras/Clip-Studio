Clip Studio

Clip Studio is a free desktop app for Windows. It turns your own gaming recordings into short vertical clips with captions and music. If you connect your TikTok account, it uploads the clips you export to your own TikTok.

How the TikTok connection works

  1. You sign in to TikTok once and allow Clip Studio to upload videos.
  2. When you export clips, Clip Studio uploads each one with its caption and hashtags.
  3. Your login stays on your computer. Clip Studio has no servers and collects no data.
